The account may also contain a user-identified rental for separate work. The independent owned-instance guard reads account credit once per minute after instance creation. This cannot guarantee provider availability or eliminate charge-posting delay. The guard stays armed until owned-instance absence is verified; separate rentals are never targets. Credit checks cannot disarm the provider guard before instance creation.

Final financial closure requires the campaign instance to be absent. The exactly attributed separate rental may remain active. Unknown contracts still prevent commitment release until attribution is resolved. Existing lifetime, price, memory, numerical, rehearsal and artifact gates remain unchanged.
